Summer me vs School me

Present Simple: affermativa, negativa, domande e avverbi di frequenza. Parliamo dell'estate come abitudine, non come racconto.

Leggi le frasi del prof. Cosa cambia tra estate e scuola?

In summer…

  • I usually get up at 9:30.
  • I never set an alarm.
  • I often have lunch at the beach.
  • I don't check my emails.
  • I am never in a hurry.

At school…

  • I always get up at 6:45.
  • I have breakfast in five minutes!
  • I usually have lunch at 2:30.
  • I check my emails every day.
  • I am often in a hurry.

Adverb before the verb: I never get up early.

Adverb after am / is / are: I am never late.

✗ My mum never be late.   ✓ My mum is never late.
