📋 Quick Rules — CAN / CAN'T ▼ show
TUTTE LE PERSONE I / you / he / she / it / we / they can play · can't play
DOMANDA Can you swim? — Yes, I can. / No, I can't.
⚠️ NO -s: she cans · NO do/does: Do you can? · NO to: I can to swim · verbo sempre base: she can plays
LEVELS ✓✓✓ really well · ✓✓ well · ✓ quite well · ✗✗ not very well (I can't … very well) · ✗✗✗ not at all (I can't … at all)
PRONUNCIATION
can debole /kən/ → I can play can forte /kæn/ → Yes, I can! can't forte /kɑːnt/ → I can't play

🗣️ Free Practice

Activity 1 — Speaking: Talent Show Judge👥 Pairs
A è il giudice di un talent show sportivo. Fa 5 domande con Can you…? e dopo ogni "Yes" chiede How well?. B risponde con un livello. Alla fine A dice: "You're in!" o "Sorry, you're out!". Poi si scambiano.
Can you play tennis? → Yes, I can.
How well? → I can play really well!
Can you ski? → No, I can't. I can't ski at all.
Activity 2 — Speaking: Report to the class🙋 Individual
Usa i risultati dell'Ability Survey. Presenta il tuo compagno alla classe con 3 frasi: una cosa che sa fare benissimo, una che sa fare abbastanza, una che non sa fare. Usa but.
Marco can swim really well, but he can't ski at all.
He can play football quite well.
Activity 3 — Writing: Me and my family✏️ Individual
Scrivi 6 frasi su di te e sulla tua famiglia: 3 con can, 3 con can't. Usa almeno 2 livelli diversi e almeno 1 avverbio (slowly, fast, well, badly…).
I can play volleyball really well.
My dad can't dance at all!
My sister can swim fast.
