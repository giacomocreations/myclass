📋 Quick Rules — Quantifiers ▼ show
COUNTABLE a burger → two burgers · many · a few · How many…?
UNCOUNTABLE meat, cheese, bread, water, money, time, homework, advice → no plurale, no a/an · much · a little · How much…?
SOME frasi affermative → There are some burgers. / There is some cheese.
ANY negative e domande → There aren't any cows. / Is there any ketchup?
A LOT OF con tutti i nomi, soprattutto in frasi affermative: I eat a lot of chocolate. (I eat much chocolate)
MUCH / MANY soprattutto in negative e domande: There isn't much bread. · Are there many burgers?
A FEW / A LITTLE piccola quantità: There are a few burgers. · There's a little bread.
ENOUGH prima del nome: There isn't enough bread! (bread enough)
🇬🇧≠🇮🇹 advice, information, homework, news sono non numerabili in inglese: I've got some advice — an advice, advices

🗣️ Free Practice

Activity 1 — Speaking: Party Planners👥 Pairs
Organizzate la festa di fine anno per 20 compagni. A controlla cosa c'è già (inventato!), B decide cosa comprare. Usate How much / How many, some / any, enough e almeno 4 contenitori diversi. Alla fine leggete la vostra shopping list alla classe.
How many bottles of water have we got? → Only a few. We need six bottles.
Is there any cake? → No, there isn't any. Let's buy two cakes.
Have we got enough cups? → No, we haven't got enough. We need a packet of paper cups.
Activity 2 — Speaking: Report to the class🙋 Individual
Usa i risultati del Food Habits Survey. Presenta il tuo compagno con 3 frasi: una con a lot of, una con a few / a little, una negativa con much / many / any. Collega con but. Attenzione alla -s della terza persona!
Sara drinks a lot of water, but she doesn't eat much fruit.
She eats a few packets of crisps a week.
She doesn't drink any fizzy drinks.
Activity 3 — Writing: My school✏️ Individual · 15 min
Scrivi 4 cose belle e 4 cose brutte della tua scuola (SB p.92 ex.6, versione potenziata). Usa almeno: some, any, much, many, a few o a little, enough.
There are some great teachers.
There aren't any vending machines.
There isn't enough space in the gym.
We've got a few new computers, but we haven't got much time to use them.
