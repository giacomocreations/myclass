📋 Quick Rules — Have to / Must ▼ show
HAVE TO soggetto + have to / has to + verbo base
I / you / we / theyI have to studyI don't have to studyDo you have to study?
he / she / itShe has to studyShe doesn't have to studyDoes she have to study?
SHORT ANSWERS Do you have to…? — Yes, I do. / No, I don't. · Does he have to…? — Yes, he does. / No, he doesn't.
MUST uguale per tutte le persone, senza to: I must try · She must try · negativa: mustn't → You mustn't judge the others.
SIGNIFICATO must / have to = è obbligatorio ✅ · mustn't = è vietato ⛔ · don't have to = non è necessario, ma puoi farlo 🆓
→ You mustn't run in the corridor. (è vietato) · You don't have to wear a uniform. (non serve)
MUST o HAVE TO? must = lo sento io come un dovere: I must do my best! · have to = una regola che viene da fuori: I have to wear a uniform at school.

🗣️ Free Practice

📺 In classe: Activity 1 a gruppi di 4 e poi tutti insieme (le regole migliori vanno in bacheca); Activity 2 con 2 volontari davanti alla LIM (toccate 🎲 per l'argomento), poi a coppie con il compagno di banco; Activity 3 sul quaderno.
🖥️ A coppie: Activity 1 e 2 a voce con il compagno (A e B si scambiano), Activity 3 sul quaderno, ognuno per conto suo.
Activity 1 — Speaking: Kindness Charter 🤝👥 Pairs👥 Groups
Come nel ritornello, scrivete le regole della gentilezza per la vostra classe: 2 regole con must / have to, 2 con mustn't e 1 con don't have to. Poi leggetele ad alta voce: gli altri dicono se sono d'accordo — "I agree!" / "I don't agree!"
We must listen to each other. We have to help new students.
We mustn't laugh at mistakes. We mustn't judge the others.
We don't have to be perfect!
Activity 2 — Speaking: Interview 🎤👥 Pairs🙋 2 volunteers
A intervista B. Tocca 🎲 per l'argomento: A fa almeno 4 domande con Do you have to…?, B risponde con la risposta breve e aggiunge un dettaglio. Alla fine A racconta alla classe in 3ª persona: "Marco has to… He doesn't have to…". Poi scambiatevi.
Do you have to help at home? — Yes, I do. I have to lay the table every evening.
Do you have to cook dinner? — No, I don't. My dad cooks.
→ Marco has to lay the table, but he doesn't have to cook dinner.
Tocca 🎲 per l'argomento dell'intervista!
Activity 3 — Writing: My verse ✏️✏️ Individual
Scrivi una nuova strofa della canzone: 4 domande con le risposte brevi (2 con you, 2 con he / she). Poi aggiungi 2 versi sul tuo futuro: uno con I have to e uno con I don't have to.
Do you have to give up your dreams? No, I don't!
Does she have to tell the truth? Yes, she does!
I have to study hard, but I don't have to be the best!
