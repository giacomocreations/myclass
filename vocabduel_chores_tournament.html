⚡ Each round gets harder · 3 questions per match · ⭐ Final → 5 questions
  1. Round of 32 & Round of 16 – What's the chore? One missing word: ___ my bed → make · do the ___ → ironing
  2. Quarterfinals – Write the chore · Level 1 A full definition + the picture + the first letters: write the whole chore. c____ t__ t____ → clear the table (not just clear)
  3. Semifinals – Write the chore · Level 2 A full definition, no help: write the whole chore. Wrong? Try again with the hint (first letters) — then it's worth ½ point
  4. Final – ⭐ Chore Challenge Harder definitions, no help, 5 questions. Same rule: the hint only after a first try, for ½ point
